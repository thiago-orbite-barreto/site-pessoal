About Me

I'm Thiago Orbite Barreto. I am driven by curiosity, a deep reading of the world, and a continuous desire to learn and share knowledge.

My journey began with a degree in Philosophy at USP (University of São Paulo), which shaped my passion for deep analysis, critical thinking, and a mindful attention to people and their complexities. To me, untangling conceptual knots and finding clarity amid noise is a skill for life.

This humanistic and reflective background naturally flows into my daily practice. Over time, I have applied this same analytical mindset and organizational focus to mediating dialogues, managing information, and improving processes across operational and administrative environments.

  • Technology & Data: Enthusiastic about the impact of innovation on human interactions and everyday problem-solving.
  • Languages & Communication: Fluent in English with a constant fascination for clear language and precise content editing.
  • People & Processes: I believe true efficiency only exists when it serves the well-being and growth of the people involved.

(In professional environments, I translate this mindset into organization, teamwork, document management, and a strong focus on user experience.)
